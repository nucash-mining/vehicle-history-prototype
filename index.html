VinTrace

Free vehicle history from official US government data.

Data: NHTSA vPIC, Recalls, Complaints & NCAP. Stolen check via NICB VINCheck.

Point at the VIN barcode on the door jamb or windshield.

AI summary

Paste an Anthropic API key to get an AI-written summary on each report. The key is stored only on this device and sent only to Anthropic. Each summary costs about 2 cents. Get a key at console.anthropic.com.
